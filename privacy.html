Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

Refined Life Co. ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what we collect on refinedlifeco.com, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Email signups. When you join our newsletter, the Soulrise waitlist, or RSVP to a gathering, we collect your email address.
  • Inquiries. When you send a gathering or workshop inquiry, we collect your name, email, and the details you share (type of event, number of guests, and your message).
  • Orders. When you buy from the Ritual Shop, checkout is handled by Stripe. Stripe collects your payment and shipping details; we never see or store your full card number.
  • Your cart. Items in your cart are saved in your own browser so they are still there when you come back. This stays on your device.

How we use it

  • To send the emails you asked for, such as inspiration, offers, waitlist updates, and gathering details.
  • To reply to your inquiry and plan your event.
  • To process, ship, and support your orders.

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Services we use

  • Kit manages our email list and sends our emails.
  • Formspree receives inquiry forms and forwards them to us by email.
  • Stripe processes payments.

Each of these services handles your information under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

  • Every email we send has an unsubscribe link. You can leave the list at any time.
  • You can ask us to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you by emailing therefinedlifeco@gmail.com.

Children

This site is for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Questions? Email therefinedlifeco@gmail.com.
